انضمام للمجموعة
You're welcome in the Data Communication course (2026)

This course introduces students to Analog and Digital Data Transmission, Transmission Media, Modulation Techniques, Data Encoding, Asynchronous and Synchronous Transmissions, Data Link Configuration and Control, Error Control, Multiplexing and De-Multiplexing, CSMA/CD, ALOHA, CSMA/CA, Ethernet technology.

Textbook: Data Communications and networking, 6th Edition, Behrouze A. Forouzan, McGraw-Hill, 2013
